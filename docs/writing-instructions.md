# Writing instructions

Use a voice-preserving, intent-faithful editorial adaptation.

Keep the author's diction, bluntness, emphasis, examples, and causal reasoning.
Change only what is needed for clarity and the target document's structure. Do
not abstract, professionalize, sanitize, or summarize the text. Do not turn
explanations of why something exists into instructions.

Keep descriptive rationale separate from operational rules.

When the author asks for minimal changes, use minimum-transform editing: retain
the original wording wherever it already works and make only necessary local
edits.
